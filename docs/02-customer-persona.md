# 1. Primary Customer

# The Stuck Adult Musician

The core PianoOS customer is an adult who wants to play piano but feels blocked by traditional learning methods.

They are not necessarily beginners.

Many have touched a piano before.

They have musical curiosity but lack confidence.

---

# 2. Demographics

Typical characteristics:

Age:

35-65

Lifestyle:

- Professional
- Retired
- Career transitioning
- Creative hobbyist

Financial:

Able and willing to invest in personal growth.

Technology:

Comfortable using:

- Apps
- YouTube
- AI tools
- Digital products

---

# 3. Emotional Profile

The customer feels:

## Excitement

"I always wanted to learn piano."

## Frustration

"I bought the keyboard, but now what?"

## Confusion

"There are thousands of tutorials and I don't know where to start."

## Embarrassment

"I should be better by now."

## Desire

"I just want to sit down and play something beautiful."

---

# 4. Common Failed Experiences

## Traditional Lessons

Problems:

- Too structured
- Too slow
- Too focused on reading
- Not enough enjoyable music

---

## Piano Apps

Problems:

- Follow notes without understanding
- Limited song choices
- Child-oriented experience
- Difficult progression

---

## YouTube

Problems:

- No curriculum
- No personalization
- Random lessons
- No feedback loop

---

# 5. Jobs To Be Done

The customer wants to:

- Play recognizable songs
- Understand chords
- Accompany singing
- Create arrangements
- Improve confidence
- Enjoy practicing

---

# 6. Core Motivations

Primary motivation:

"I want to make music."

Not:

"I want to complete lessons."

---

Secondary motivations:

- Personal achievement
- Creativity
- Relaxation
- Family entertainment
- Self-expression

---

# 7. Customer Language

They say:

"I just want to play songs."

"I don't care about becoming a concert pianist."

"I wish someone would explain how musicians actually think."

"I know what sounds good, but I don't know how to create it."

---

# 8. Customer Promise

PianoOS promises:

You do not need to become a traditional pianist to become a musician.

You need to understand the language of music.

---

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** PianoOS targets adults seeking practical musicianship rather than children learning traditional piano.

**Reason:** The strongest product differentiation exists in adult musical confidence.
