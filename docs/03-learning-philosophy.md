# 1. Philosophy Statement

PianoOS teaches piano as a language.

The goal is not simply pressing correct keys.

The goal is understanding:

- What music is doing
- Why songs sound the way they do
- How musicians create

# 3. Core Beliefs

## Music Before Mechanics

Students should experience success quickly.

The first goal:

"I can make music."

---

## Songs Are The Teacher

Songs demonstrate:

- Chord movement
- Rhythm
- Emotion
- Structure

---

## Theory Has Purpose

Theory is introduced when it explains something meaningful.

Example:

Do not teach:

"Here is a major seventh chord."

Teach:

"That beautiful emotional sound in ballads comes from this chord."

---

# 4. Skills PianoOS Develops

## Harmonic Understanding

Students learn:

- Chords
- Progressions
- Keys
- Modulation

---

## Rhythmic Understanding

Students learn:

- Patterns
- Grooves
- Accompaniment

---

## Ear Development

Students learn:

- Recognizing chords
- Predicting movement
- Playing by ear

---

## Creativity

Students learn:

- Improvisation
- Arrangement
- Expression

---

# 5. Role of Sheet Music

PianoOS does not reject sheet music.

Sheet music is one musical skill.

However:

Reading should support musicianship, not become the barrier preventing musicianship.

---

# 6. The Emotional Goal

The student should experience:

"I understand music."

Not:

"I survived another lesson."
