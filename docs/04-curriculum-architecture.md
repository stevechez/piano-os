# 1. Curriculum Overview

PianoOS curriculum follows the progression:

Understand
↓
Play
↓
Create

---

# 2. Learning Path

## Stage 1 — Piano Foundations

Goal:

Become comfortable with the instrument.

Lessons:

- Keyboard geography
- Notes
- Octaves
- Intervals
- Basic rhythm

Outcome:

"I understand the piano layout."

---

# Stage 2 — Chord Language

Goal:

Understand the foundation of songs.

Lessons:

- Major chords
- Minor chords
- Seventh chords
- Chord inversions
- Common progressions

Outcome:

"I understand how songs are built."

---

# Stage 3 — Song Construction

Goal:

Learn real music.

Lessons:

- Left hand patterns
- Right hand melodies
- Chord charts
- Song structures

Outcome:

"I can play recognizable songs."

---

# Stage 4 — Musical Expression

Goal:

Move beyond copying.

Lessons:

- Dynamics
- Arrangement
- Improvisation
- Personal style

Outcome:

"I can make a song my own."

---

# Stage 5 — Advanced Musicianship

Goal:

Develop independence.

Lessons:

- Advanced harmony
- Creating arrangements
- Playing by ear
- Composition

Outcome:

"I think like a musician."

---

# 3. Lesson Structure

Every lesson should contain:

## Concept

What are we learning?

## Demonstration

Show musical application.

## Practice

Apply skill.

## Song Connection

Use the concept in music.

---

# 4. Song-Based Learning

Songs are not rewards after learning.

Songs are the learning environment.

---

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** Curriculum is built around progressive musicianship rather than isolated technical exercises.

**Reason:** Adults stay motivated when progress connects directly to music.
