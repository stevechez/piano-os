# PianoOS — Song Learning Engine

**Document:** 07-song-learning-engine.md  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation

---

# 1. Vision

Songs are the primary learning environment inside PianoOS.

Students should learn musical concepts through real music rather than isolated exercises.

---

# 2. Teaching Philosophy

Every song teaches:

- Chords
- Rhythm
- Harmony
- Technique
- Expression

Songs are lessons.

Lessons are songs.

---

# 3. Song Structure

Each song should include:

## Overview

- Artist
- Genre
- Difficulty
- Estimated practice time

---

## Musical Concepts

The lesson identifies:

- Key
- Chords
- Progressions
- Patterns
- Rhythm

---

## Step-by-Step Learning

Students progress through:

1. Chords
2. Left hand
3. Right hand
4. Rhythm
5. Full arrangement

---

## Understanding

The lesson explains:

Why this progression sounds emotional.

Why this chord creates tension.

Why this rhythm works.

---

# 4. Multiple Arrangements

Each song should support:

Beginner

Intermediate

Advanced

Students grow with the same music.

---

# 5. AI Arrangements

Future versions should allow:

"I want this easier."

"I only know basic chords."

"Teach this using broken chords."

"Create a worship version."

"Make this sound like Jim Brickman."

---

# 6. Learning Outcomes

Students should finish every song understanding:

- What they played
- Why it worked
- How to reuse the idea elsewhere

---

# 7. Long-Term Vision

Students stop memorizing songs.

Students begin recognizing musical patterns across songs.

---

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** Songs are the curriculum rather than rewards after completing lessons.

**Reason:** Adults remain engaged when learning directly through music.
