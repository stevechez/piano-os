# PianoOS — Business Model

**Document:** 09-business-model.md  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation

---

# 1. Mission

Build a sustainable business while helping adults become musicians.

Revenue should support continuous improvement of curriculum and technology.

---

# 2. Target Customer

Primary:

Adults interested in learning piano through practical musicianship.

---

# 3. Pricing Philosophy

Students should experience meaningful value before paying.

The free experience should create confidence.

Premium unlocks personalization.

---

# 4. Initial Pricing Hypothesis

## Free

- Piano Foundations
- Introductory lessons
- Limited songs

---

## PianoOS Pro

Monthly subscription.

Potential features:

- Full curriculum
- Song library
- Practice plans
- AI Coach
- Progress tracking

---

## Future Premium

Potential additions:

- Live workshops
- Masterclasses
- Song packs
- Community
- Personalized coaching

---

# 5. Additional Revenue

Potential future opportunities:

- Song collections
- Genre packs
- Seasonal content
- Printable resources
- Merchandise
- Affiliate partnerships

---

# 6. Success Metrics

Measure:

- Practice consistency
- Lesson completion
- Musical confidence
- Subscription retention

Not simply:

- Time spent inside the app

---

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** Optimize for long-term student success rather than maximizing engagement metrics.

**Reason:** Better musicians become long-term customers.

**Date:** July 2026
