# PianoOS — Launch Strategy

**Document:** 10-launch-strategy.md  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Foundation

---

# 1. Vision

PianoOS will launch as an educational platform for adults who want to understand music and play confidently.

The launch should emphasize transformation rather than features.

---

# 2. Initial Audience

Primary:

Adults who:

- Bought a keyboard
- Quit traditional lessons
- Want to play songs
- Feel stuck

---

# 3. Core Message

Learn piano by understanding music.

Not by memorizing notes.

---

# 4. Marketing Themes

Topics:

- Chords
- Music theory made simple
- Playing by ear
- Improvisation
- Song breakdowns
- Piano myths
- Adult learning

---

# 5. Content Strategy

Publish consistently:

YouTube

Short-form video

Blog articles

Email newsletter

Practice tips

Song demonstrations

---

# 6. Community

Encourage students to:

- Share progress
- Request songs
- Celebrate milestones
- Learn together

---

# 7. MVP Goals

Validate:

- Teaching philosophy
- Customer demand
- Retention
- AI coaching usefulness

Avoid feature expansion before validation.

---

# 8. Long-Term Vision

PianoOS becomes the trusted learning platform for adults who want practical musicianship.

The community should associate PianoOS with:

- Confidence
- Creativity
- Understanding
- Enjoyment

---

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** Launch around the educational philosophy rather than competing on song count or features.

**Reason:** The learning approach is PianoOS's primary differentiator.

**Date:** July 2026
