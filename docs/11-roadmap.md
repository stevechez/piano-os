# Purpose

This roadmap defines the long-term evolution of PianoOS.

It is intentionally strategic rather than task-oriented.

Each phase should produce a complete, valuable product increment before moving to the next phase.

The roadmap is expected to evolve as customer feedback is gathered.

---

# Product Vision

PianoOS will become the operating system for adult piano learning.

The platform will combine:

- Structured curriculum
- Practical musicianship
- AI coaching
- Personalized practice
- Song-based learning
- Creative development

The goal is to help adults become confident musicians.

---

# Success Criteria

The roadmap is successful when a student can progress from:

> "I've never really played piano."

to

> "I understand music and can confidently play the songs I love."

# Phase 1 — Foundation

## Goal

Build the educational foundation of PianoOS.

---

## Objectives

Create:

- Product architecture
- Design system
- Authentication
- User profiles
- Curriculum framework
- Lesson engine
- Progress tracking
- Initial onboarding

---

## Deliverables

- Landing page
- User accounts
- Dashboard
- First-time experience
- Lesson player
- Practice dashboard

---

# Phase 2 — Piano Foundations

## Goal

Teach students how the instrument works.

---

## Curriculum

Topics include:

- Piano geography
- Keyboard navigation
- Octaves
- Intervals
- Rhythm fundamentals
- Finger positioning
- Hand coordination

---

## Outcome

Students become comfortable sitting at the piano.

---

# Phase 3 — Chord Language

## Goal

Teach the language behind modern music.

---

## Topics

- Major chords
- Minor chords
- Seventh chords
- Suspended chords
- Chord inversions
- Diatonic harmony
- Common progressions

---

## Outcome

Students understand how songs are constructed.

---

# Phase 4 — Song Learning Engine

## Goal

Transform songs into structured learning experiences.

---

## Features

Song lessons include:

- Chord breakdown
- Musical analysis
- Step-by-step instruction
- Difficulty levels
- Practice checkpoints

---

## Outcome

Students learn songs while developing transferable skills.

---

# Phase 5 — AI Piano Coach

## Goal

Introduce personalized instruction.

---

## Features

AI Coach can:

- Recommend lessons
- Build practice plans
- Answer questions
- Explain theory
- Adapt difficulty
- Suggest songs

---

## Outcome

Every student follows a personalized learning path.

---

# Phase 6 — Practice Intelligence

## Goal

Improve practice quality.

---

## Features

- Daily practice plans
- Goal tracking
- Practice history
- Weakness detection
- Intelligent recommendations

---

## Future

Potential integration with MIDI keyboards for automatic feedback.

---

- Chord recognition

---

# Guiding Principles

Every phase should:

- Produce real customer value.
- Stay aligned with the Product Principles.
- Improve musical understanding.
- Build confidence.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.

---

# Non-Goals (Current)

The following are intentionally out of scope for the MVP:

- Social networking
- Competitive leaderboards
- Child-focused curriculum
- Certification programs
- Large-scale marketplace
- Virtual concerts
- AR/VR experiences
- Complex DAW integration

These may be revisited in the future but should not delay the core learning experience.

---

# Product Milestones

## Milestone 1

A new student completes onboarding and successfully plays their first recognizable song.

---

## Milestone 2

A student understands enough harmony to recognize common chord progressions across multiple songs.

---

## Milestone 3

The AI Coach provides personalized practice plans based on student goals and progress.

---

## Milestone 4

Students can connect a MIDI keyboard and receive real-time feedback.

---

## Milestone 5

Students confidently improvise, arrange songs, and continue learning independently.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

Build slowly.

Teach deeply.

Validate continuously.

Never sacrifice educational quality for feature quantity.

Every release should make students better musicians.

---

> **North Star**

> PianoOS should become the first application adults recommend when someone says:

> "I've always wanted to learn piano, but I don't know where to start."
