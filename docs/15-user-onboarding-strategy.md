# Purpose

This document defines the PianoOS onboarding experience.

The purpose of onboarding is not simply to collect user information.

The purpose is to create immediate confidence and demonstrate the PianoOS learning philosophy.

Within the first session, the user should understand:

- This is different from traditional piano apps.
- I can make progress quickly.
- This approach fits how I want to learn.

---

# Core Onboarding Principle

## The First Experience Must Create Musical Confidence

Most piano learners do not quit because they cannot learn.

They quit because they lose confidence.

The first session should create the feeling:

> "Maybe I actually can learn piano."

---

# Target User Mindset

The typical new user arrives thinking:

"I bought a piano and I still don't know where to start."

"I tried lessons before and got frustrated."

"I don't have time to practice like a child."

"I just want to play songs I love."

The onboarding experience should acknowledge this.

---

# Onboarding Goals

The onboarding process should:

1. Understand the user.
2. Identify their goals.
3. Create a personalized pathway.
4. Deliver an early musical success.
5. Establish trust.

---

# Desired Time To First Value

Target:

## Less than 10 minutes

The user should experience meaningful progress during the first session.

---

# Step 1 — Welcome

## Objective

Create immediate emotional connection.

Message:

"Welcome to PianoOS.

Learn piano by understanding music—not memorizing notes."

---

Avoid:

"You are about to begin lesson one."

Prefer:

"Let's discover how you learn music."

---

# Step 2 — Musical Background

## Objective

Understand the student's history.

Questions:

- Have you played piano before?
- Did you take lessons?
- Do you play another instrument?
- Can you read music?

---

Possible answers:

Beginner:

"I have never played."

Returning:

"I played years ago."

Self-taught:

"I know some chords."

Musician:

"I play another instrument."

---

# Step 3 — Learning Goal

## Objective

Understand motivation.

Question:

"What do you want to do with piano?"

Options:

- Play songs for myself
- Play for friends and family
- Accompany singing
- Write songs
- Improve musical understanding
- Become a better musician

---

# Step 4 — Music Preferences

## Objective

Create emotional personalization.

Questions:

"What music inspires you?"

Examples:

- Pop
- Ballads
- Movie music
- Worship
- Jazz
- Classical
- Singer-songwriter

---

Question:

"Who are some artists you love?"

Examples:

- Jim Brickman
- Yiruma
- Coldplay
- Adele
- Beatles

---

# Step 5 — Practice Reality

## Objective

Respect adult schedules.

Question:

"How much time can you realistically practice?"

Options:

- 10 minutes/day
- 20 minutes/day
- 30 minutes/day
- A few times per week

---

# Step 6 — Personalized Path

After onboarding, create a learning path.

Example:

## User Profile

Experience:

Beginner

Goal:

Play emotional songs

Favorite artists:

Jim Brickman, Yiruma

Practice:

20 minutes/day

---

Generated path:

```
Piano Foundations

↓

Chord Language

↓

Emotional Ballad Techniques

↓

Your First Piano Arrangement
```

---

# Step 7 — First Musical Success

The first lesson should not begin with theory.

It should begin with music.

---

Example:

Lesson:

"Your First Beautiful Piano Chord"

Student learns:

- C Major
- Simple pattern
- Emotional progression

Immediately hears:

"I am creating music."

---

# First Session Experience

The user should complete:

- One concept
- One exercise
- One musical pattern
- One recognizable sound

---

# Personalization Principles

The system should personalize:

## Content

Which lessons appear first.

---

## Songs

Which examples are shown.

---

## Explanations

How concepts are explained.

---

## Practice

How much is assigned.

---

# Returning User Experience

Returning users should immediately see:

"Continue your journey."

Not:

"Start another lesson."

---

Dashboard should show:

- Today's practice
- Current goal
- Recent progress
- Recommended next step

---

# Onboarding Mistakes To Avoid

## Mistake 1

Starting with sheet music.

Why:

Creates immediate friction.

---

## Mistake 2

Asking too many questions.

Why:

Creates fatigue before value.

---

## Mistake 3

Treating adults like children.

Why:

Damages trust.

---

## Mistake 4

Showing the entire curriculum.

Why:

Creates overwhelm.

---

# AI Role During Onboarding

AI should assist by:

- Creating personalized pathways
- Explaining recommendations
- Encouraging confidence

Example:

"You mentioned loving emotional piano.

We'll focus on chords and patterns that create that sound."
