# Purpose

This document defines the PianoOS brand strategy and market positioning.

The goal is to establish a clear identity that differentiates PianoOS from traditional piano learning platforms.

---

# Brand Vision

PianoOS exists to help adults become confident musicians.

We believe people do not quit piano because they lack ability.

They quit because traditional methods often teach the wrong thing first.

---

# Category Position

## Current Category

Online piano lessons.

---

## PianoOS Category

AI-powered practical musicianship.

---

# Positioning Statement

For adults who want to learn piano but feel frustrated by traditional methods, PianoOS is an AI-powered musical learning platform that teaches piano through chords, patterns, songs, and understanding instead of forcing students to start with traditional notation.

Unlike traditional piano apps, PianoOS helps students understand why music works so they can play, adapt, and create.

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** Position PianoOS around understanding music rather than learning songs.

**Reason:** Song libraries are easy to compare; learning philosophy is the differentiation.

**Date:** July 2026

---

## Decision 002

**Decision:** Target adult learners rather than children.

**Reason:** Adult learners are underserved by existing piano education experiences.
