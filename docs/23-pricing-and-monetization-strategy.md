# Purpose

This document defines the PianoOS pricing and monetization strategy.

The goal is to create a sustainable business while aligning pricing with the value of helping adults become confident musicians.

---

# Core Pricing Philosophy

## Charge For Transformation, Not Content Access

PianoOS is not selling:

- Videos
- Lessons
- Exercises
- Songs

PianoOS is selling:

- Musical understanding
- Personalized guidance
- Faster progress
- Confidence
- Independence

---

# Market Context

Traditional alternatives:

---

# Private Piano Teacher

Typical cost:

$50–150 per lesson

Limitations:

- Fixed schedule
- Expensive
- Limited personalization between lessons

---

# Online Piano Apps

Typical cost:

$10–30/month

Limitations:

- Generic pathways
- Limited understanding
- Song-following approach

---

# Online Courses

Typical cost:

$100–500+

Limitations:

- Static content
- No personal feedback

---

# PianoOS Opportunity

Position between:

Affordable app

and

Expensive private instruction

---

# Initial Pricing Hypothesis

MVP pricing should validate willingness to pay.

---

# Free Tier

Purpose:

Allow users to experience the teaching method.

Includes:

- Basic introduction
- First lessons
- Limited AI interaction
- Sample songs

Goal:

Create trust.

---

# PianoOS Core

Target:

$19–29/month

Includes:

- Full curriculum
- Song pathways
- Practice system
- AI learning support

Target customer:

Committed adult learner.

---

# PianoOS Pro

Target:

$39–59/month

Includes:

Everything in Core plus:

- Advanced AI coaching
- Personalized practice plans
- Deeper progress insights
- MIDI integration when available
- Premium song pathways

# Pricing Psychology

The customer question should not be:

"How much is a piano app?"

The customer question should be:

"Is this worth less than the lessons I never took?"

---

# Trial Strategy

A trial should focus on experiencing transformation.

Avoid:

Giving access to everything.

Instead:

Create a guided first experience.

---

# Example Trial

Day 1:

Play your first chord progression.

Day 3:

Understand song structure.

Day 7:

Play your first recognizable arrangement.

Day 14:

See your personalized growth path.

---

# Conversion Strategy

Users should upgrade because they believe:

"I am actually learning."

Not because:

"I need more content."

# Pricing Risks

---

# Risk 1

Pricing Too Low

Problem:

Signals low value.

Solution:

Position as a teacher replacement.

---

# Risk 2

Pricing Too High Before Validation

Problem:

Creates unnecessary friction.

Solution:

Validate with early users.

---

# Risk 3

Competing On Features

Problem:

Larger companies can copy features.

Solution:

Compete on teaching philosophy.

---

# Long-Term Vision

PianoOS becomes the default platform for adults who always wanted to play piano.

The value proposition:

"For less than one private lesson, you get a personal musical guide every day."

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** PianoOS should be positioned above basic piano apps.

**Reason:** The product value comes from personalized learning and transformation.

**Date:** July 2026

## Decision 002

**Decision:** Subscription is the primary business model.

**Reason:** Musical growth is a long-term journey requiring continuous guidance.
