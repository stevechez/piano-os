# Purpose

This document defines the go-to-market strategy for PianoOS.

The goal is to attract adults who want to learn piano through a different approach.

The strategy focuses on:

- Founder credibility
- Educational content
- Community building
- Demonstrating the teaching philosophy

---

# Core GTM Philosophy

## Teach Before Selling

PianoOS should not begin by saying:

"Buy our piano course."

It should begin by saying:

"Here is why learning piano feels harder than it should."

---

# Market Opportunity

The target audience is not people looking for "piano lessons."

The audience is people who:

- Bought a keyboard and feel stuck
- Quit lessons years ago
- Want to finally learn
- Love music but feel intimidated
- Believe they are "not musical"

---

# Primary Customer Segment

## The Adult Piano Dreamer

Profile:

Age:

35–65

Characteristics:

- Has disposable income
- Values hobbies and personal growth
- Loves music

# Founder-Led Marketing Strategy

The founder story is a major asset.

Core story:

"I became an accomplished pianist by understanding chords, patterns, and music—not by becoming a traditional sight reader.

I built PianoOS because I believe many adults can learn piano if they are taught differently."
