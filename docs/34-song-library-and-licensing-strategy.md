# Purpose

This document defines the strategy for building the PianoOS song library.

The goal is not to create the largest song catalog.

The goal is to create the most effective learning catalog.

---

# Core Principle

## Songs Are Learning Vehicles

A song should exist in PianoOS because it teaches something valuable.

Not simply because it is popular.

---

# Traditional Approach

Many piano apps optimize for:

```
Popular Song

↓

User Plays Notes

↓

Song Completion
```

---

# PianoOS Approach

```
Musical Concept

↓

Song Selection

↓

Understanding

↓

Application

↓

Creative Ability
```

---

# Song Library Philosophy

Every song should answer:

"Why is this song in the curriculum?"

---

# Song Selection Criteria

A PianoOS song should have:

---

# 1. Musical Value

The song teaches:

- Chords
- Harmony
- Rhythm
- Arrangement
- Expression

---

# 2. Emotional Connection

The learner should think:

"I love this song."

---

# 3. Appropriate Difficulty

The song should have:

- Beginner entry point
- Progressive versions

---

# 4. Transferability

The concepts should apply elsewhere.

---

Example:

Learning:

Adele-style ballad accompaniment

should teach:

- Broken chords
- Dynamics
- Emotional phrasing

---

# Song Categories

---

# Category 1

## First Songs

Purpose:

Create confidence.

Characteristics:

- Simple chords
- Recognizable melodies
- Immediate success

Examples:

- Four chord songs
- Simple ballads

---

# Category 2

## Emotional Piano

Purpose:

Match the PianoOS audience.

Characteristics:

- Beautiful harmony
- Expressive playing
- Arrangement focused

Examples:

- Contemporary piano ballads
- Cinematic pieces

---

# Category 3

## Popular Songs

Purpose:

Motivation.

Characteristics:

- Songs users actually want to play

---

# Category 4

## Musician Builder Songs

Purpose:

Develop skills.

Characteristics:

- Interesting chord progressions
- Patterns
- Improvisation opportunities

# Licensing Reality

Commercial music requires rights.

Potential rights:

---

# Mechanical Rights

Required for:

- Reproducing compositions

---

# Synchronization Rights

Required for:

- Video/audio pairing

---

# Master Rights

Required for:

- Original recordings

---

# PianoOS Approach

Avoid unnecessary complexity early.

---

# MVP Options

---

# Option 1

## Original Curriculum Songs

Advantages:

- Full ownership
- No licensing issues
- Educational control

---

Disadvantage:

Less immediate emotional connection.

---

# Option 2

## Licensed Songs

Advantages:

- User motivation
- Familiar music

---

Disadvantages:

- Cost
- Complexity

---

# Option 3

## Hybrid Model

Recommended.

Use:

- Original teaching pieces
- Public domain music
- Licensed songs where justified

---

# Song Strategy Risks

---

# Risk 1

Trying to compete on catalog size.

Solution:

Compete on learning value.

---

# Risk 2

Licensing costs too early.

Solution:

Validate learning method first.

---

# Risk 3

Choosing songs adults do not care about.

Solution:

Let user preference guide selection.

---

# Final Vision

The PianoOS song library is not a collection.

It is a carefully designed musical journey.

Every song moves the student closer to independence.

---

# Decision Log

## Decision 001

**Decision:** Song selection is curriculum-driven, not catalog-driven.

**Reason:** The product goal is musicianship.

**Date:** July 2026

---

## Decision 002

**Decision:** MVP uses a focused hybrid song strategy.

**Reason:** Balance emotional connection with business reality.

**Date:** July 2026

---

## Decision 003

**Decision:** The first goal is transformation, not song quantity.

**Reason:** A smaller powerful library creates stronger learning outcomes.
