# Purpose

This document defines the business model strategy for PianoOS.

The goal is to create a sustainable business that aligns customer value with ongoing learning progress.

---

# Business Thesis

PianoOS is not selling:

"Access to piano videos."

PianoOS is selling:

"A personalized path to becoming a confident piano player."

---

# Market Reality

Adults already spend money on:

- Private lessons
- Online courses
- Piano apps
- Music books
- YouTube memberships
- Instruments

The opportunity is creating a better learning system.

---

# Primary Customer

## Adult Piano Learner

Characteristics:

- Owns or plans to own a keyboard/piano
- Has disposable income
- Values hobbies and self-improvement
- Wants meaningful progress

---

# Customer Pain

Common frustrations:

"I bought a piano but don't know what to do."

"I tried apps but quit."

"I don't have time for traditional lessons."

"I want to actually play songs."

---

# Value Proposition

PianoOS provides:

- Structured learning
- Practical musicianship
- Personalized guidance
- Song application
- AI coaching

---

# Subscription Philosophy

## Sell Progress, Not Content

Users should pay because:

"I am becoming a better musician."

Not:

"I have access to more lessons."

---

# Recommended Pricing Model

---

# Free Tier

Purpose:

Create trust.

Includes:

- Account creation
- Basic assessment
- Intro lessons
- Limited AI interactions

Goal:

Experience the methodology.

---

# PianoOS Plus

Recommended:

$19–29/month

or

$199/year

---

Includes:

- Full curriculum
- Song library
- Practice tracking
- AI Coach
- Progress system

---

# PianoOS Premium

Future:

$49–79/month

Includes:

- Advanced AI coaching
- Performance feedback
- Personalized arrangements
- Live workshops
- Community features

---

# Why Subscription Works

Piano learning is not a one-time transaction.

Progress requires:

- Practice
- Guidance
- Feedback
- New challenges

---

# Annual Pricing Strategy

Annual plans should be encouraged.

Reason:

Learning piano is a long-term journey.

# Retention Strategy

PianoOS must avoid churn caused by:

"I finished the lessons."

---

The system should provide:

- New songs
- New skills
- New challenges
- New goals

---

# Engagement Loop

```
Learn Concept

↓

Apply To Song

↓

Feel Progress

↓

Unlock Next Challenge

↓

Continue Learning
```

---

# Conversion Strategy

Free users should experience:

The first breakthrough.

Example:

Within first session:

"I understand chords."

"I can play something."

---

# Pricing Psychology

Avoid competing with:

Cheap apps.

---

Position against:

- Private lessons
- Courses
- Frustration

---

# Business Risks

---

# Risk 1

Users subscribe but do not practice.

Solution:

Build accountability and progress loops.

---

# Risk 2

Pricing too low.

Solution:

Position around transformation.

---

# Risk 3

High content costs.

Solution:

Build reusable learning frameworks.

The operating system for adult piano learning.
